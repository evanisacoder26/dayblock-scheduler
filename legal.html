Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

This page explains what Dayblock does with your information and the rules for using it, in plain language.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

  • We store only what Dayblock needs: your name, email, a scrambled (hashed) password, and your plans.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text involved is sent to Google's Gemini AI to produce the answer.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Dayblock website and app ("Dayblock", "we", "us").

What we collect

InformationWhy we have it
Name and email addressTo create your account, show your name, and send you sign-in codes.
PasswordTo let you log in. We store only a one-way hash, never the password itself.
Your plans: tasks, events, times, notes, priorities and brain-dump textThis is the planner itself, saved to your account so it syncs across devices.
Sign-in codesStored only as a hash and deleted after use or within 30 minutes.
Login sessions and trusted devicesTo keep you logged in and remember devices where you chose "Don't ask for a code". This includes a device label based on your browser type (for example "Chrome on Windows") and dates.
Technical data such as IP addressHandled by our hosting and database providers to deliver the site and protect it from abuse (for example, limiting repeated login attempts).

We don't collect payment details, location, contacts or anything from your device beyond what's listed here. If you use the planner without an account, your plan stays in your own browser and never reaches us.

How we use your information

  • To run Dayblock: save and sync your plans, and show your dashboard.
  • To keep your account secure: sign-in codes, login sessions, and alerts such as "your email was changed".
  • To provide AI features when you use them (see below).

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build profiles about you, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

AI features

The AI brain dump, event suggestions, schedule advice and the chat assistant are powered by Google Gemini. When you use one, we send Google the text needed to answer: your notes or message, and the relevant part of your schedule (event titles, times, categories, priorities and notes). We don't send your name, email or password.

Google processes this under its own terms. Depending on the service plan, Google may keep or review this content, including to improve its products. Please don't put passwords, health details or other sensitive information into tasks, notes or chat messages. AI features only run when you use them.

Cookies and browser storage

Dayblock uses your browser's storage only for things it needs to work: keeping you logged in, remembering a trusted device, saving the plan if you use the planner without an account, and remembering your Light/Dark choice. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and plans: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes: until used, or at most 30 minutes.
  • Login sessions: until you log out, or log out your other devices from Settings.
  • Trusted devices: 30 days, or until you forget them in Settings or change your password.

When you delete your account, your account, plans, sessions and trusted devices are deleted immediately. Copies held by our providers (for example in backups) are removed on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

From Settings you can, at any time:

  • See and change your name, email and password.
  • Download a copy of your account details and all your plans.
  • Log out other devices and forget trusted devices.
  • Delete your account and everything in it.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as asking what data we hold or objecting to how it's used. To make a request, contact us.

Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes. Every new login needs a code emailed to you, and the database only releases your plans to a login that passed that check. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don't use elsewhere.

Children

Dayblock isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we'll give notice on the site before they take effect.
