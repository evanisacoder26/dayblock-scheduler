Features

Everything you need to plan the day, and nothing you don't.

Dayblock does the tedious part of planning, so you spend your time doing the work.

AI brain dump to schedule

Paste or type a messy list. AI splits it into tasks, estimates each one, sets priority and category, and places them in open slots without double-booking. Tasks with a time ("call at 3") land right where you said.

24-hour drag-and-drop timeline

Move blocks, stretch them to resize, tap to mark done. Color-coded for deep work, admin, meetings and personal time.

Smart event suggestions

Type an event title and AI suggests how long it'll take, how important it is, and a free time that fits around your day.

An assistant that warns you

Flags overloaded days and long stretches with no break, and offers to move low-priority tasks to tomorrow in one tap.

Reschedule in one click

Running late? Reschedule Remaining re-packs unfinished tasks from right now. Meetings stay where they are.

Focus Mode

Dims everything except the task happening now, with the minutes left, so the rest of your day can wait.

Dashboard and weekly insights

See today at a glance, planned vs. done hours for the week, and where your time actually goes.

Synced everywhere

Your plan saves to your account automatically. Pick it up on your phone, laptop or anywhere you sign in.

How it works

From scattered thoughts to a plan in under a minute.

Step 1

Dump it all out

Type tasks the way you think of them. Durations and times are optional; "call mom tonight" is fine.

Step 2

Let AI schedule it

One click turns the list into timed blocks, most important first, fitted around what's already booked.

Step 3

Adjust and get going

Drag things around, tick them off as you go, and reschedule the rest when the day changes.
