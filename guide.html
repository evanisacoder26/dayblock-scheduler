🚀Getting started#

Dayblock turns a messy list of things to do into a planned day. Here's the fastest way to get going. Tick steps off as you go; your progress is saved in this browser.

🧠Brain dump & Auto-Schedule#

Type tasks the way you'd say them. AI splits them up, estimates how long each takes, picks a priority and category, and fits them into free time.

  1. Open the planner. On a phone, tap the Plan tab.
  2. Type into Brain dump: one task per line, or separated by commas.
  3. Select Auto-Schedule with AI, or press Ctrl + Enter.
Try it: brain dump

This demo uses simple keyword rules. The real planner uses AI, which understands much more.

Tips for better results

  • Add a duration when you know it: “2 hrs”, “45m”, “half an hour”.
  • Add a time for fixed things: “call with Sam at 3pm” is placed at 3:00 PM.
  • Say how important it is: “urgent”, “today”, or “maybe” and “if time” for low priority.
💡

Auto-Schedule keeps to working hours (8 AM to 8 PM) and puts the most important tasks first. Late in the evening it uses the rest of the evening instead. Anything that doesn't fit goes to the backlog.

✏️Creating & editing events#

Add events one at a time, with an AI helper that fills in the details.

  1. Select + New event, click an empty spot on the timeline, or press N.
  2. Type a title, for example “Write launch email”.
  3. Optional: select Suggest with AI. It proposes a duration, category, priority and a free time that fits your day, with a reason. Select Apply to use it.
  4. Adjust anything, then select Create event.

To change an event later, click its pencil icon (or press and hold on a phone). If the time you pick overlaps another event, the form tells you and offers the nearest free time.

🗑️Deleting & Undo#

Delete with the trash icon on a block or backlog item, the Delete key on a selected block, or Delete in the edit form.

Every delete shows “Deleted ‘…’ · Undo” for a few seconds. Undo puts the event back where it was. If you've filled that slot in the meantime, it goes to the nearest free time instead.

✅

Changes made by the AI assistant also have their own Undo button in the chat.

📥The backlog#

Tasks that aren't on the timeline yet: things that didn't fit, or that you unscheduled.

  • Place drops a task into the next free slot.
  • Drag a task onto the timeline to choose the time yourself.
  • Tasks are sorted by priority, with High first.

🎯Focus Mode & rescheduling#

Focus Mode

Turn on Focus Mode in the top bar to dim everything except the task happening right now, with the minutes left. It follows the clock, so it works on today's schedule.

Reschedule Remaining

Running behind? Reschedule Remaining re-packs every unfinished task from the current time. Meetings stay where they are, since other people are involved, and finished tasks stay as a record.

The assistant card

In the sidebar, the assistant card warns you about an overloaded day, three or more hours without a break, and high-priority backlog tasks that would fit. It can move low-priority tasks to tomorrow in one click.

✦The AI assistant#

Review or change your schedule by chatting. Open it with ✦ Ask the assistant (bottom right), or the Assistant tab on a phone.

How it keeps you safe

  • Every change appears in the chat as a list, with an Undo button.
  • It can't double-book you. If a time is taken, it picks the nearest free slot and says so.
  • If your request is unclear (“move the meeting” when there are two), it asks which one.
  • It asks before deleting more than three things.
⚠️

AI can make mistakes. Check important changes. If you see “Google's AI is overloaded”, wait a minute and try again.

🔐Sign-in codes & security#

Every new login needs your password and a 6-digit code sent to your email.

  • Codes expire after 10 minutes and work only once. After 5 wrong tries, start again.
  • Didn't get it? Wait a minute and select Resend code, and check your spam folder.
  • Tick Don't ask for a code on this device for 30 days on your own computer or phone to skip the code next time.

See the Privacy Policy for how your data is stored and protected.

❓FAQ & troubleshooting#

I didn't receive my sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder, wait a minute, then select Resend code. Make sure you typed your email correctly. If you still get nothing, the site's email service may only be set up for certain addresses while Dayblock is in testing.

It says “Google's AI is overloaded”

Google's AI is temporarily busy. Wait a minute and try again. Your plan isn't affected. Everything except the AI features keeps working.

Is my plan saved? Will it sync to my phone?

Yes. When you're logged in, every change saves to your account automatically (the top bar shows “All changes saved”) and appears on any device you log in from.

The site looks out of date after an update

Press Ctrl + F5 (or Cmd + Shift + R on a Mac) to reload the newest version.

Dark mode isn't applying to a page

Choose Light or Dark in Settings → Appearance, then reload the page with Ctrl + F5. The setting is saved per device, so choose it on each one.

The AI put a task at the wrong time

Drag it where you want it, or ask the assistant to move it. You can make results more precise by including durations and times in your brain dump, like “2 hrs” or “at 3pm”.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account, enter your password, type DELETE and confirm. Download your data first if you want a copy.

Something else is wrong

Report it on the project's GitHub Issues page. Describe what you did and what happened.
